Northern Italy & Slovenia

Turin → the Langhe wine hills → Verona → the Dolomites → Lake Bled → Ljubljana. A slow, indulgent arc of wine, mountains, and opera under the stars — built around your love of Italy.

This is a slow, indulgent arc from the elegant arcades of Piedmont to the emerald shores of the Julian Alps — a journey stitched together by wine, mountains, and an opera under the stars. It begins in Turin's chocolate-and-vermouth cafés, drifts into the rolling, UNESCO-listed hills of the Langhe where Barolo and Moscato are born, then climbs east through Verona's Roman romance and into the cathedral-spire peaks of the Dolomites.

The final act crosses into Slovenia, where a fairy-tale island floats on Lake Bled and Ljubljana's dragon-guarded riverfront glows at dusk. It's built for couples who measure a trip in long lunches, golden-hour drives, and views that make you forget to check your phone.

The journey, day by day

Eleven Days of Wine & Mountains

Turin and the Langhe, an opera night in Verona, three nights in the Dolomites, then over the border to Slovenia.

1
Turin

Baroque grandeur & vermouth twilight

Arrive by train into Torino Porta Nuova. Wander the porticoes of Via Roma into the ceremonial Piazza San Carlo — the city's “drawing room” — for an aperitivo of Turin-born vermouth. Dinner at the gilded 18th-century Del Cambio sets the tone for the whole trip.

2
Turin

Royal city, Mole & Egyptian treasures

Ride the lift inside the Mole Antonelliana, Turin's improbable spire, then the Museo Egizio — the most important Egyptian collection outside Cairo. Stroll Piazza Castello and the Royal Palace, ducking into a café for bicerin, the city's layered espresso-chocolate-cream drink.

3
Drive → the Langhe

Hilltop castles & first vines

Collect the car and drive ≈ 90 minutes south into the Langhe, the wave-like wine hills now under UNESCO protection. Check into Casa di Langa, then orient at La Morra's belvedere — the postcard sweep across the Barolo hills — and the candy-striped Cappella del Barolo.

4
Langhe · the heart

A full Barolo wine day

Visit the WiMu wine museum in Barolo's castle, taste at an estate beneath Serralunga d'Alba, lunch at Osteria dell'Arco in Alba, and wander the perfectly preserved medieval village of Neive. End with a golden-hour Moscato d'Asti back near La Morra. Most cellars are by appointment — book ahead.

5
Drive → Verona

Truffles, then east to Verona

A morning in Alba — hazelnut-and-truffle markets, the cathedral, the old towers — then the scenic ≈ 3.5-hr transfer east. Arrive in Verona and check into Hotel Gabbia d'Oro, a romantic warren of antiques off Piazza delle Erbe. An evening passeggiata with a Valpolicella nightcap.

6
Verona · the centrepiece

Juliet's balcony & opera in the Arena

Wander Piazza delle Erbe and Piazza dei Signori, visit Casa di Giulietta, climb Torre dei Lamberti. Then the night belongs to the 2,000-year-old Arena di Verona, where — under open sky — you'll see grand opera (Aida, Carmen, Nabucco) as candles flicker across the stone tiers. Dinner at Antica Bottega del Vino.

7
Drive → the Dolomites

Alpine ascent to Val Gardena

A spectacular ≈ 3-hr drive trades vineyards for vertical limestone — the “Pale Mountains” that glow rose-pink at sunset. Settle into the Adler Spa Resort in Ortisei and spend the afternoon in the thermal pools as the peaks turn gold, with a first taste of South Tyrolean cooking.

8
Dolomites

Alpe di Siusi & the Seceda ridge

Cable-car up to the Alpe di Siusi, Europe's largest high meadow, for blissful walking beneath the Sassolungo massif. In the afternoon, the lifts to the Seceda ridgeline and its dramatic tilted spires. Lunch is a rifugio affair — bread dumplings, barley soup, apple strudel — then an evening spa soak.

9
Drive → Slovenia

Tre Cime, then over the border

Begin with the Dolomites' icon, the three monolithic towers of Tre Cime di Lavaredo. Then point the car southeast through Cortina's valleys into Slovenia's Julian Alps, arriving at Vila Bled — Tito's former lakeside summer residence — for a first walk as the island church floats into view.

10
Lake Bled

Island church, clifftop castle & a gorge

Take a pletna boat to Bled Island, climb the 99 steps and ring the wishing bell. Up to clifftop Bled Castle for the definitive panorama and a slice of kremšnita cream cake. In the afternoon, the turquoise boardwalks of Vintgar Gorge (seasonal). Toast the day with Slovenian sparkling wine.

11
Ljubljana · the finale

Dragons, the Triple Bridge & a castle table

Drive 40 minutes to Ljubljana and the riverside Zlata Ladjica. Walk the Plečnik-designed Triple Bridge, cross the Dragon Bridge, browse the Central Market, then ride the funicular up to end the journey at Strelec, in the castle's Archer's Tower — a tasting menu beneath medieval stone with the city glittering below.

Where you'd lay your head

Inns, Palazzi & Alpine Spas

A Belle Époque townhouse, a vineyard resort, a Baroque jewel-box, an alpine spa, and a president's former villa.

Opera35

$$–$$$
Turin

A 19th-century Belle Époque building in the leafy, café-lined San Salvario quarter near the Po. Frescoes and stucco survive alongside crisp contemporary design, with a hidden courtyard for rare city-centre quiet. Intimate and unmistakably not a chain.

Casa di Langa

$$$$
Langhe wine country · most romantic

A contemporary luxury resort on a 100-hectare estate of its own vineyards and hazelnut groves in the heart of Barolo country. Infinity pool, spa, and the Michelin-recognized Fàula restaurant, with floor-to-ceiling windows framing the vine-striped horizon.

Hotel Gabbia d'Oro

$$$
Verona

A jewel-box tucked into a Baroque palazzo steps from Piazza delle Erbe. Antiques, frescoed ceilings, gilt mirrors and a roof terrace make it feel like an aristocratic family home — sumptuous, slightly theatrical, and walking distance to the Arena.

Adler Spa Resort Dolomiti

$$$$
Ortisei, Val Gardena

An expansive alpine spa resort with the Sassolungo and Seceda peaks as backdrop. The draw is the spa — indoor-outdoor thermal pools and saunas with mountain views — plus warm Tyrolean rooms and a half-board larder of Italian-Austrian cooking.

Vila Bled

$$$–$$$$
Lake Bled

The former summer residence of Yugoslav president Tito, in a lakeside park with a private beach and direct views to Bled Island. History soaks the walls — Khrushchev, Nasser and Indira Gandhi were all guests — with a unique, cinematic period aura.

Zlata Ladjica

$$–$$$
Ljubljana

The “Golden Little Boat,” a small boutique right on the Ljubljanica river in the pedestrian old town, steps from the Triple Bridge. Intimate and warmly designed — the ideal final-night base, walkable to everything.

Tables worth booking

Where You'd Eat

Piedmont's truffle-and-Barolo cooking, a Veronese wine temple, a mountain hut, and a tasting menu in a castle tower.

01

Del Cambio

Turin · Piedmontese fine dining

A gilded 18th-century landmark where Cavour himself dined — now Michelin-starred, the historic salons gloriously intact.

Order: vitello tonnato · agnolotti del plin · finanziera · a glass of Barolo
02

Osteria dell'Arco

Alba · Traditional Langhe

Run by the team behind the birthplace of Slow Food — honest, regional cooking on Alba's main piazza.

Order: tajarin with butter (and truffle in season) · brasato al Barolo · carne cruda
03

Antica Bottega del Vino

Verona · Veneto, wine-focused

An atmospheric, wood-panelled institution with one of Italy's deepest cellars — perfect before or after the opera.

Order: pastissada de caval · risotto all'Amarone · an Amarone or Valpolicella
04

Mountain Rifugios

Dolomites · South Tyrolean alpine

Sun-deck huts on the Alpe di Siusi and beneath Sassolungo serve hearty, view-drenched lunches mid-hike.

Order: canederli (speck dumplings) · barley soup · Kaiserschmarrn & apple strudel
05

Strelec

Ljubljana · Modern Slovenian tasting menu

Set in Ljubljana Castle's round Archer's Tower with sweeping city views — chef Igor Jagodic's showpiece. The perfect last supper.

Order: the chef's tasting menu (closed Sundays · reserve well ahead)

The Practical Bits

Getting there & away

Fly into Turin, or into Milan and take a fast train to Torino Porta Nuova (≈ 1 hr). Depart from Ljubljana airport. Pick the car up after the Turin days and arrange a one-way drop in Ljubljana (confirm cross-border permission at booking).

Driving notes

Historic centres (Turin, Verona, Bolzano) have ZTL camera zones — park outside and walk, or have the hotel register your plate. Buy a Slovenian motorway vinjeta sticker. Dolomite passes are gorgeous but winding — drive them by day.

Best time to go

Mid-June to early September is the sweet spot: the Verona opera season is live and the Dolomites' hiking season is at its peak (lifts running, rifugios open, meadows in bloom). September brings harvest energy and thinner crowds.

Weather & packing

Hot, humid lowlands (Turin, Verona often 30 °C+) and cool alpine air in the Dolomites and Bled, with possible afternoon mountain storms. Pack layers, a rain shell, good walking shoes — and something smart-casual for the opera and the big dinners.

Book early

The bottlenecks all sell out in high summer: Arena opera seats, Strelec, Langhe winery visits, and Casa di Langa / Adler Spa. Reserve the headline experiences weeks ahead.

The spirit of it

Build in unhurried lunches — the long Langhe table and a rifugio sun-deck are as much the trip as any museum. Leave the schedule loose enough to linger.

Loved for

Romance, world-class scenery, and the best food and wine of the three — the trip you'd plan for a once-in-a-while celebration.

The trade-off

The most expensive and the busiest in peak summer, and the most logistics (a rental car, ZTL zones, by-appointment bookings).
